# Product

## Overview

**BookPilot** is a SaaS platform for **omnichannel appointment booking** for service businesses.

It allows businesses to manage bookings through:

- WhatsApp
- phone calls
- a public booking website

It also includes a **web dashboard** for internal operations.

Customers can book, cancel, or reschedule appointments through conversation or a web flow. Businesses can manage schedules, staff, services, availability, and bookings from a single system.

The product is designed to be **generic and configurable**, so it can be adapted to multiple service industries without changing the core platform.

---

## Core idea

BookPilot is not just a chatbot and not just a scheduling dashboard.

It is a **booking operating system** with three customer-facing entry points:

- conversational booking through WhatsApp
- conversational booking through phone calls
- direct booking through the web

All channels connect to the same central booking engine and the same source of truth.

---

## Problem

Many service businesses still manage bookings manually through:

- phone calls
- WhatsApp messages
- Instagram DMs
- paper calendars
- fragmented digital tools

This creates common problems:

- missed bookings outside business hours
- delayed replies to customers
- lost sales opportunities
- scheduling mistakes
- double bookings
- high manual workload
- poor visibility into staff schedules and availability
- no unified booking history

Small businesses often cannot provide the same level of responsiveness and consistency as larger companies.

---

## Solution

BookPilot gives service businesses a unified system to manage bookings across web, WhatsApp, and phone calls.

The system should allow businesses to:

- receive booking requests 24/7
- automatically offer available time slots
- create bookings in a consistent way
- let customers cancel or reschedule
- keep a unified schedule across all channels
- manage staff, services, and availability from one dashboard
- reduce manual work and missed opportunities

The goal is to make a small business feel operationally stronger, faster, and more reliable.

---

## Who the product is for

BookPilot is intended for **service businesses that work with appointments or reservation-based operations**.

Examples include:

- hair salons
- barbershops
- beauty centers
- physiotherapy clinics
- small health practices
- consultants
- personal training businesses
- other appointment-based service businesses

The first versions of the product should focus on businesses with relatively straightforward booking logic, such as:

- service
- duration
- staff availability
- working hours
- rescheduling and cancellation

More complex verticals can be supported later through more advanced booking strategies.

---

## Product vision

The long-term vision is to build a **generic omnichannel booking platform** that can serve many different industries with minimal adaptation.

The system should not be designed around one specific industry. Instead, it should be designed around reusable booking concepts such as:

- organizations
- locations
- services
- staff
- resources
- availability
- bookings
- notifications
- conversations

Different industries may need different booking strategies, but the platform should keep a common core.

---

## Main product surfaces

BookPilot has four main surfaces.

### 1. Public booking website

A customer-facing booking experience where users can:

- choose a service
- optionally choose a staff member
- view available time slots
- create a booking
- provide contact details
- cancel or reschedule when supported

### 2. WhatsApp booking

A conversational booking channel where customers can:

- ask for availability
- request a service
- confirm a booking
- cancel or reschedule
- interact naturally through text or voice messages

### 3. Phone call booking

A conversational voice channel where customers can:

- call the business
- request an appointment
- confirm a booking
- cancel or reschedule

### 4. Business dashboard

An internal web dashboard where the business can:

- view the schedule
- create or edit bookings manually
- manage staff
- manage services
- manage working hours and exceptions
- manage customer records
- configure availability rules

## What businesses are buying

Businesses are not buying a chatbot and not just a dashboard.

They are buying:

- a booking system that works across channels
- a unified schedule
- better responsiveness
- less manual scheduling work
- fewer missed booking opportunities
- better operational control

The dashboard is the control panel, but the product itself is the full booking system.

---

## Customer-facing value proposition

The product should be understandable in simple terms.

A good high-level value proposition is:

**Manage bookings from your website, WhatsApp, and phone calls in one system.**

Other acceptable formulations:

- **Omnichannel booking for service businesses**
- **One booking system for web, WhatsApp, and voice**
- **AI-assisted booking and scheduling for service businesses**

---

## Internal product principles

The product should follow these principles:

1. **One source of truth**

   All bookings, availability, and scheduling decisions must be consistent across channels.

2. **One central booking engine**

   Web, WhatsApp, and phone calls must use the same scheduling logic.

3. **Generic core**

   The platform should avoid being hardcoded for a single industry.

4. **Configurable, not custom-built per customer**

   Businesses should adapt the product through configuration, not through bespoke implementations.

5. **Operational reliability over flashy automation**

   The most important thing is that bookings are correct, availability is accurate, and businesses can trust the system.

---

## What the product is not

BookPilot is not:

- just a WhatsApp bot
- just a voice bot
- just a calendar UI
- a custom automation flow per client
- a single-industry app hardcoded for one business type
- a marketing site with a thin booking widget
- a no-code workflow product at its core

External tools and integrations may be used, but the core scheduling and business logic must belong to the platform.

---

## Initial scope

The initial product should focus on appointment-based businesses with booking logic similar to:

- service + duration
- optional staff assignment
- working hours
- availability lookup
- booking creation
- rescheduling
- cancellation
- dashboard-based management

Initial scope should prioritize:

- web booking
- WhatsApp booking
- dashboard operations
- reusable scheduling logic
- strong data consistency

Phone call booking may be part of the early product vision, but the architecture should support it from the start whether or not it is implemented in the first milestone.

---

## Out of scope for the earliest phase

The earliest phase should avoid trying to solve every vertical immediately.

Examples that are likely out of scope at the beginning:

- restaurant table allocation
- advanced multi-resource medical workflows
- marketplace-style discovery
- deep CRM features
- advanced billing and invoicing
- custom enterprise workflows per customer

These can be added later if the core product proves strong.

---

## Product success criteria

A strong early version of BookPilot should allow a business to:

- configure services and schedules
- receive booking requests from multiple channels
- create bookings consistently without conflicts
- let customers cancel or reschedule
- manage everything from one dashboard

A strong customer experience should feel like:

- fast responses
- clear available slots
- low friction booking
- trust that the booking is real and correctly recorded

A strong business experience should feel like:

- fewer interruptions
- less manual scheduling work
- one consistent agenda
- confidence in availability and booking state

---

## Current focus

The current product focus is:

- a strong generic booking core
- web booking
- WhatsApp booking
- dashboard operations
- consistent availability and booking state

The architecture must support voice and phone booking from the beginning, even if some voice features are implemented later.

Avoid expanding early implementation scope into highly specialized vertical logic unless explicitly requested.

BookPilot is a **generic omnichannel booking platform** for service businesses.

It combines:

- a public booking website
- WhatsApp booking
- phone-based booking
- a business dashboard
- a central booking engine

The core promise of the product is simple:

**one system to manage bookings across web, WhatsApp, and voice.**
